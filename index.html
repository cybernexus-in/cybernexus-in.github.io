What we do
End-to-end fraud investigation
APK & malware analysis
We reverse-engineer suspicious APKs to identify what data they steal, what permissions they abuse, and which servers they send your information to.
Stolen data assessment
Through forensic investigation, we determine exactly what was compromised - cards, bank logins, OTPs, SMS, passwords - and provide a detailed exposure report.
Malware removal guidance
We identify malicious apps on your device and guide you step-by-step through safe removal - stopping SMS forwarding, revoking permissions, and verifying your phone is clean.
Bank account recovery assistance
We help you report fraud to your bank, guide you through the national cyber crime helpline (1930), assist with blocking compromised cards, and walk you through securing your accounts.
Court-ready evidence packaging
Every finding is documented in a structured forensic report with Section 63 BSA compliant documentation - ready for FIR filing, cybercrime.gov.in submission, or court proceedings.
Cyber cell complaint filing
We help you file complaints on cybercrime.gov.in (National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal), draft the complaint with technical evidence, and coordinate with local cyber police stations.
